Title: Warranty Ready

Notes / Description:
Built a reusable warranty-intake readiness screen with two fixed document lanes. A designated claimant declares purchase and condition records, GenLayer validators return a compact PRESENT, MISSING, or CONFLICT code for each lane, and the contract derives readiness deterministically. One supplement is allowed, while the issuer alone routes the package. It never decides coverage, fault, payment, repair, replacement, eligibility, or legal rights.
